## What the service is

The service is a free educational platform covering probability, statistics and quantitative finance. It includes lessons, exercises on five levels, simulations, an in-browser Python notebook, interview practice, the “Debunk Lab” (a mathematical check of trading claims) and a weekly newsletter. The service shows no adverts and offers no paid plans.

## Using the service without an account

Without an account you can read all materials and solve exercises. Your progress is then stored only in your browser’s storage (localStorage) on your device and does not reach us. If you later sign in, you can merge that progress into your account; only then is it sent to our server.

## What data we process and why

### Account

You sign in with a link sent by e-mail or with a Google or GitHub account. We store your e-mail address, username and optional display name, language, time zone, daily goal, learning goal, self-reported level and placement-test result, and whether your profile is public and whether you have opted out of leagues.

### Learning and activity

We store lesson progress, notes, highlights, bookmarks and saved notebooks; exercise attempts (answers, scores, time taken, hints used); review cards; XP, streaks, achievements and league membership; interview practice sessions and answers; and certificates (name, track, date). A certificate can be publicly verified by its identifier.

### Community and reports

We process your discussion posts, shared solutions, votes and reports, as well as content-error reports, claims submitted to the Debunk Lab and disputes of AI grades. We also store your notification preferences and browser push subscriptions (endpoint address and keys). Staff members are assigned roles.

### Newsletter

The “Problem of the week + Debunk” newsletter uses double opt-in: after you enter your address we send an e-mail with a link, and you confirm by clicking a button on the page. We store your e-mail address, language and the dates of confirmation and unsubscription. The subscriber list is kept in our own database, not at the e-mail provider. Every issue has a one-click unsubscribe link.

### AI grading of answers

When the feature is enabled, open answers may be graded by a language model. We send Anthropic (Claude API) only the answer text, the question and the grading rubric, without your e-mail or name. We log usage (tokens, cost) against your account to apply quotas. A grade has no legal effect on you; you can dispute it, and a moderator reviews the dispute.

### Campaign counter

Visits from links with `utm_*` parameters are counted as daily totals per source, medium, campaign and landing page. We store no IP address, cookie or user identifier.

### Security and abuse prevention

We apply rate limits keyed by user identifier or IP address. The counters last from a few minutes to one day and are not combined with any other data. Bot detection (Vercel BotID) runs on sign-in, newsletter sign-up, AI features and anonymous submissions. We moderate content (hiding posts, holding new accounts’ posts, bans), keep an audit log of staff actions and check for unusual XP gains. Such cases are only flagged for human review.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account in Settings, under “Delete account”, by typing your username. The account and all private data are deleted at once, including certificates. Community posts and shared solutions remain readable but without the author’s name.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- access your data and take it with you: in Settings, “Download my data” creates a JSON file with everything stored for your account;
- rectify your data in Settings;
- have your data erased by deleting your account;
- restrict processing and object to processing based on legitimate interest;
- withdraw consent at any time: the unsubscribe link in the newsletter, the cookie settings page, your browser’s notification settings. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing;
- lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (PUODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, Poland.
