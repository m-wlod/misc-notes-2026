## Educational materials

All content in the service, including lessons, exercises, simulations, notebooks, the newsletter and Debunk Lab entries, is purely educational. It is not investment advice or an investment recommendation within the meaning of the Polish Act on Trading in Financial Instruments or Regulation (EU) No 596/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council on market abuse (MAR). It does not take into account your financial situation, goals or appetite for risk.

## No signals

The service publishes no trading signals and no guidance on what, when or at what price to buy or sell. Such content may not be posted in the community either.

## Simulations and models

Simulations are mathematical models with stated assumptions and random seeds. They show how a model behaves under those assumptions. They are not market forecasts or a promise of any result. Changing the assumptions or the seed can lead to entirely different outcomes. Past results do not guarantee future results.

## Risk

Investing and trading involve a risk of loss, including the loss of all the money invested. You make financial decisions on your own and at your own risk. Where needed, seek help from a licensed adviser.

## Debunk Lab

The Debunk Lab assesses claims made in public, not the people who made them. A verdict concerns a specific claim in its context and as at the date of publication. How we check and correct content is described in the Corrections policy.
