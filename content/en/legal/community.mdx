## Why these guidelines exist

Discussions in the service are for learning together: asking questions, comparing solutions and explaining difficult points to one another. These guidelines are meant to keep them factual, kind and free of adverts. They complement the Terms of service; by taking part in discussions, you accept both documents.

## Where discussions happen

Discussions sit under lessons and exercises. An exercise’s thread opens only after you solve it or reveal the solution, so that nobody’s exercise is spoilt.

## What we expect

- Be kind to others, even when they are wrong. Criticise arguments, not people.
- Stay on the topic of the lesson or exercise the thread belongs to.
- Show your reasoning; a good question is as valuable as a good answer.
- Write maths between dollar signs, e.g. `$p^2$`, and code in backticks, e.g. `` `np.mean(x)` ``.

## What we do not publish

- adverts and promotional content;
- trading signals and buy or sell tips;
- referral and affiliate links;
- invitations to private channels, groups and messengers;
- other people’s personal data;
- spam, including repeating the same content.

The Terms of service and the Disclaimer also apply.

## New accounts

Links in posts from new accounts are shown as plain text. Some posts, especially from new accounts, wait for a moderator’s review before they become visible.

## Moderation

Moderators can hide a post. The author still sees a hidden post, but others do not. For serious or repeated breaches, a moderator can ban an account temporarily or permanently. Moderators’ actions are recorded in a log.

## Reporting posts

If a post breaks these guidelines, use the “Report” button next to it. The report goes to the moderators.
