## What cookies and browser storage are

Cookies are small files your browser saves on your device. The service also uses browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage). Data in localStorage stays on your device and is not sent to us unless you sign in and merge your progress into your account.

We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

## Categories

- **Essential**: needed for the service to work, always on.
- **Functional**: remember your settings and progress on your device.
- **Analytics**: planned (PostHog, EU cloud); runs only with your consent.
- **Videos**: the YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com) and TikTok players set their own cookies only once a video is loaded, that is after you click it or choose “remember my choice”.

## How to change your settings

On the cookie settings page you can switch analytics and videos on or off at any time. Essential items are always active. You can also delete cookies and site data in your browser settings; you will then lose any progress saved without an account.
