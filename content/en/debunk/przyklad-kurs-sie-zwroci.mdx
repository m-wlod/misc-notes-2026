<TheMath>

<Depth level="easy">
If everyone must bring three people, the "floors" grow: 3, 9, 27, 81… After a dozen or so floors you need more people than live in Poland. Most participants are always on the bottom floor, with nobody left to invite.
</Depth>

<Depth level="formal">
Level $n$ holds $r^n$ people and the whole scheme up to level $n$ holds $\sum_{i=0}^{n} r^i = \frac{r^{n+1}-1}{r-1}$. The bottom level's share tends to $\frac{r-1}{r}$: about two thirds for $r = 3$.
</Depth>

</TheMath>

<Checker id="mlm" />

<WhatToDoInstead>
Judge a course by what it teaches and what can be checked, not by a promise that referrals will pay for it.
</WhatToDoInstead>
