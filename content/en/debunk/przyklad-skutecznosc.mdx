<TheMath>

<Depth level="easy">
What counts is the average result of one trade, not how often you win. If you win 10 in 9 trades out of 10 and lose 100 in the tenth, you lose on average.
</Depth>

<Depth level="formal">
The expected value of a trade is $\mathbb{E} = p\,W - (1-p)\,L - c$. With $p = 0.9$, $W = 10$, $L = 100$, $c = 1$ it is $\mathbb{E} = -2$. Break-even is $p^* = \frac{L + c}{W + L} \approx 0.918$.
</Depth>

</TheMath>

<Checker id="ev" />

<WhatToDoInstead>
Ask for the average win, the average loss and the costs, not just the win rate. Work out the expected value before trusting a percentage.
</WhatToDoInstead>
